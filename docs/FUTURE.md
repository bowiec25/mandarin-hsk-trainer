# Future extensions

Ideas for after v1 (or later milestones), so they don't get lost. Each line says **what**, **why**, and **what already makes it easier**. When one gets planned into a milestone, move it to `docs/PLAN.md` and mark it here.

| Idea | Why | Already makes it easier | Where it would go |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grader: typo rule can accept the opposite** (found 2026-11-03, M4 Step 4b) | The grader accepts "expensive" for 便宜 (cheap): it's 2 letters from "inexpensive", and long answers allow 2 typos. A prefix like "in-"/"un-" flips the meaning | Grader tests + the judge's eval cases (`tests/data/judge_eval_hsk.jsonl`) show such cases | A grader rule (no typo match that only adds/removes a leading "in"/"un"/"dis"), or a scan of `answers.jsonl` for answer pairs this close |
| **Writer redo keeps banked sentences** (found 2026-11-04, M4 Step 5) | After a `PROMPT_VERSION` bump, `sentence_writer` replaces a word's whole line, so sentences banked from play would be lost | The bank and the writer share `sentences.jsonl` (ADR 0011); every sentence is re-checked on load | Before the next prompt bump: on redo, keep the old sentences that still pass and add the new ones (up to the cap) |
| **Missed sentences come back sooner** (added 2026-11-04, ADR 0011) | A banked sentence you got wrong is picked again sooner than "least recently seen" | ADR 0011 tracks when each banked sentence was shown; M5's `progress_store` logs right/wrong | M5: weight the banked pick by past mistakes |
| **Smart "what's next" deck** (added 2026-11-02) | Study what you need: words you often get **wrong** come back more, words seen **recently** wait, words you know well fade out | `deck_builder` is its own block (M3 Step 3), so only it changes; M5's `progress_store` logs every attempt (right/wrong, when) | M5: `progress_store` + Leitner boxes, then a smarter `deck_builder` |
| **Mistake log / stats** (added 2026-11-02) | See your weak words, your accuracy per band, and your progress over time | Same attempt log as above | M5: `hsk stats` |

| **Fold judge verdicts into the answer list** (added 2026-11-02, ADR 0005) | Answers the judge accepted become instant, offline list answers | The judge saves every verdict in `data/judge_cache.jsonl` | A small script + your review, then re-commit `answers.jsonl` |

| **Call the dictionary text `meanings`, not `answers`** (added 2026-11-03, deferred from M4 Step 4a to keep the step simple) | In `cards.jsonl` the CC-CEDICT text still sits in a field called `answers`, but since ADR 0006 the quiz's answers come only from `answers.jsonl` (the CLI swaps them in).
